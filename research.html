Research

Research

My research makes power systems with high shares of stochastic, low-inertia generation more reliable, frequency-secure and resilient.

Research themes

Frequency-secure scheduling of low-inertia microgrids

Look-ahead and scenario-aware optimal scheduling that keeps frequency within limits after disturbances, with energy storage providing fast support.

TPEC 2026 · NAPS 2025

Resilience-constrained stochastic scheduling

Multi-phase stochastic scheduling of microgrids that plans for extreme events before, during and after they occur.

NAPS 2026 poster · manuscript in preparation

Reliability assessment with machine learning

Composite reliability assessment of power systems accelerated with machine learning, and reliability of VSM-based microgrids that accounts for transient stability.

NAPS 2024 · TPEC 2025

Frequency support from electric vehicles and DERs

Using electric vehicles and other distributed resources to supply frequency support in grids with little rotational inertia.

ETFG 2025

Real-time power system simulation

Electromechanical and EMT real-time simulation with OPAL-RT RT-Lab and ePHASORSIM for testing control and scheduling methods.

OPAL-RT RT-Lab · ePHASORSIM

Earlier work in Nepal

Small-signal stability with wind penetration, reactive power (VAR) compensation, distribution hosting capacity and reliability worth, and lithium-ion battery charging strategies.
