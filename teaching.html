Teaching

Nearly a decade of teaching across power systems, power electronics, control and renewable energy, at both undergraduate and graduate levels.

University of Wyoming

EE 3510 — Electric Machines and Power Systems

Undergraduate course. Delivered lectures.

EE 5885 — Reliability of Engineering Systems

Graduate course on power system reliability. Delivered lectures.

Tribhuvan University & Kathmandu University, Nepal

As Assistant Professor at the Institute of Engineering (2017–2023) and Lecturer at National College of Engineering (2016–2017), I taught more than twelve courses and supervised undergraduate and graduate research projects, several of which led to peer-reviewed publications with my students. Earlier, I was a teaching assistant at Kathmandu University and at Thapathali Campus, IOE.

From 2021 to 2023 I served as Head of the Electrical Engineering Department at Pashchimanchal Campus, leading programme management, faculty coordination and curriculum development.
